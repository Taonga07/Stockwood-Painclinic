---

**Monika treats patients with:**

- Headaches
- Migraine headaches
- Bowel disorders like Irritable Bowel Syndrome
- Chronic constipation
- Frozen shoulders
- Sports injuries
- Hiatus hernia

**Painful symptoms such as:**

- Neck pain
- Upper back pain
- Middle back pain
- Lower back pain
- Sacral pain
- Coccyx pain
- Shoulder, arm, elbow, wrist and hand pain
- Hip, knee, ankle and foot pain
- Sciatica
- Muscle, ligament or joint pain
- Pain caused by overwork
- Trauma, i.e. accidents
- Stomach pain
- Operating scar pain

**Cranial Osteopathy for Babies and Young Children:**

- Birth trauma
- Feeding difficulties
- Colic and digestive disorders
- Disturbed sleep patterns
- Hearing problems (glue ear etc.)
- Allergies (asthma, hay fever, eczema)

**Developmental delay such as:**

- Delayed speech
- Learning difficulties
- Socialising difficulties
- Attention deficit disorder
