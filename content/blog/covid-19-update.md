UPDATE

As the government is lightening the covid restrictions more and more, all immunosuppressed patients are getting more concerned about the ongoing covid numbers, that are just no longer being reported as the government has other problems to deal with. In order to keep all my patients as safe as I can possibly make it, I am continuing with my covid regime. Patients park outside and stay in their cars. Phone me and we discuss on the phone their symptoms etc (we can hear each other so much better when we don't wear a mask) and then they put on their mask, and I put on my PPE and I go out and fetch them in. This means every minute we are in the same room is spent hands-on in order to make them better.
