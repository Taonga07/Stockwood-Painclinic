Osteopathy is a safe and natural therapy that concentrates on the structure of the human body. Osteopaths use their hands to treat the muscles and joints so as to improve the mechanics of the body. Osteopaths are well known for their treatment of back pain, but improving the mechanics of the body will also relieve a wide range of conditions.
